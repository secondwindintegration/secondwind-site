What we can do for you

We take the jobs that eat your day and hand them to a helper

Everything is written in your words, and nothing goes out until you've checked it. Here are the jobs we take on most often, from building your website and online shop to the everyday admin.

New websites and online shops

We build you a clear, modern website and set up an online shop, so customers can find you and buy from you any time. Then we keep it running and up to date, so you never have to touch the technical side.

For example: we rebuilt Julia's Fabric Boutique's website and started their online shop, so their sewing patterns are now posted straight to customers' doors. See the project.

Your email inbox, sorted

Every morning your emails are sorted into orders, questions, complaints and junk, and each one has a reply drafted in your voice. You read it, change anything you like, and press send.

For example: "Do you still have the blue linen?" gets a friendly reply with the stock and price, ready to send.

Social posts and customer emails

New stock turns into Facebook and Instagram posts, plus an email to your customer list. A simple rewards program keeps your regulars coming back.

For example: snap a photo of a new arrival, and a post and an email are ready for your OK.

Paperwork and monthly reports

We connect your till, your accounts (such as QuickBooks or Xero) and your bank. Your sales, spending and best sellers are summed up in plain English every month.

For example: on the 1st of each month, a one-page summary is waiting in your inbox.

Customer questions and bookings

Common questions about opening hours, stock, classes and returns are answered straight away, day or night. Anything unusual is passed to you.

For example: a class booking is confirmed, added to your calendar and followed by a reminder.

How we work

We start with where your time goes

You don't need to know anything about AI. You just need to know your week, and we take it from there.

  1. We map your week

    We sit down for half an hour and list the jobs you do every day and every week.

  2. We find the time-wasters

    We pick the two or three jobs that take the most time, like answering the same emails.

  3. We build a helper for each

    Each helper is set up for one job, in your words, using the tools you already have.

  4. We check it with you

    For the first weeks you approve everything. Once you trust it, it simply runs, and we keep an eye on it.

Questions

Straight answers

Can't find your question? Give Jake a call.

How much does it cost?

The first conversation is free. Setup is a one-time fixed price: A$1,500 for One Helper, A$3,500 for Shop Setup, or from A$6,500 for Whole Business. Monthly care is optional, at A$199 or A$449 a month, and it's free during your support period.

How long does it take?

Most shops are set up in about three weeks, and your shop keeps trading as normal the whole time.

I'm not good with computers. Will I cope?

Yes. We do all the setup, train you patiently over a video call (or in person by arrangement), and leave you a one-page guide. Most weeks you'll just read your posts and press approve.

Do you come to my shop?

Most of our work is done over the phone and video calls, so we can help shops anywhere in Australia. We'll guide you through every step, and we can share your screen so you never feel lost. If you'd rather meet in person, just ask, and we can arrange a visit.

Will anything go out without me seeing it?

No. You approve every post and email before it's sent.

What if something stops working?

It's covered during your package's support period, and on any Care plan after that. Care Plus includes same-day phone help.

Is my customers' information safe?

Yes. Customers join your email list only if they say yes, and they can unsubscribe at any time. Your customer list belongs to you, and we never share or sell it.

Am I locked in?

No. Setup is a one-off payment, and monthly care can be stopped at any time.

Privacy policy

How we look after your information

Last updated 21 October 2026. Second Wind Integration is run by Jake McGrath, ABN 39 445 477 450. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles. This page explains, in plain English, what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • When you ask us to call you: your name, your business's name, your phone number and the best time to call.
  • When you book a chat online: your name, email address and anything you add to the booking, collected through Calendly.
  • When you sign up for our monthly tip: your email address and your consent to receive it.
  • When we work with your business: the contact details of you and your staff. We may also need access to the systems we set up, such as your email, social media, till or accounts.
  • When you visit this website: basic, anonymous visit statistics from our website host.

Why we collect it

We use your information only to reply to you, to provide and support the services you've asked for, to send you the monthly tip if you've said yes, and to run our business (for example, invoicing and our tax records).

Your business's systems and customer information

When we set up tools for your business, we may be able to see information about your customers. We only access what we need to do the job, we never sell or share it, and we never use it for our own marketing. Your customer list always belongs to you. Any email list we set up for you only adds customers who have said yes, and every email includes a way to unsubscribe, as the Spam Act 2003 requires.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only with the service providers we need to run our business, such as Google Workspace (email), Netlify (website and forms) and Calendly (bookings), plus our accounting tools, or when the law requires it. Some of these providers store information on servers outside Australia, such as in the United States. We choose providers with strong security and privacy practices.

Emails from us

We only send our monthly tip to people who have asked for it. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, or by letting us know.

How we keep it safe

We use secure, password-protected accounts with two-step sign-in wherever it's available, and we only keep information for as long as we need it. When a project ends, we remove our access to your systems if you ask us to.

Seeing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at any time. Just contact us using the details below.

Questions or complaints

If you have a question or a concern, please contact Jake on 0417 813 548 or jake@secondwindintegration.com. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
