What we can do for you

We take the jobs that eat your day and hand them to a helper

Each of these jobs is about giving you hours back. Customer questions answered, enquiries sorted, posts written and paperwork done, all in your words, and nothing goes out until you've checked it.

New websites and online shops

We build you a clear, modern website and set up an online shop, so customers can find you and buy from you any time. For example: we rebuilt Julia's Fabric Boutique's website and started their online shop, so their sewing patterns are now posted straight to customers' doors. See the project.

Your email inbox, sorted

Every morning your emails are sorted into orders, questions, complaints and junk, and each one has a reply drafted in your voice. You read it, change anything you like, and press send.

For example: "Do you still have the blue linen?" gets a friendly reply with the stock and price, ready to send.

Social posts and customer emails

New stock turns into Facebook and Instagram posts, plus an email to your customer list. A simple rewards program keeps your regulars coming back, and happy customers get a friendly nudge to leave a Google review.

For example: snap a photo of a new arrival, and a post and an email are ready for your OK.

Paperwork and monthly reports

We connect your till, your accounts (such as QuickBooks or Xero) and your bank. Your sales, spending and best sellers are summed up for you every month.

For example: on the 1st of each month, a one-page summary is waiting in your inbox.

Customer questions and bookings

We build an answer bank: saved replies for your top 10 customer questions on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and email. When a question comes in, day or night, a reply is drafted straight away and goes out once you OK it. Anything unusual is flagged for you.

For example: "Are you open Sunday?" gets a reply ready for your OK. A class booking request gets a confirmation drafted, then it's added to your calendar with a reminder.

Privacy policy

How we look after your information

Last updated 2 November 2026. Second Wind Integration is run by Jake McGrath, ABN 39 445 477 450. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles. This page explains, in plain English, what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • When you fill in a form on this website: your name, your business's name, your phone number or email, and anything you tell us about your business.
  • When you book a chat online: your name, email address and anything you add to the booking, collected through Calendly.
  • When you sign up for our monthly tip: your email address and your consent to receive it.
  • When we work with your business: the contact details of you and your staff. We may also need access to the systems we set up, such as your email, social media, till or accounts.
  • When you visit this website: basic, anonymous visit statistics from our website host. If you arrive from one of our Facebook or Instagram ads, the Meta Pixel records your visit and whether you sent an enquiry or booked a chat, so we can tell which ads work. You can control this in your Facebook ad settings.

Why we collect it

We use your information only to reply to you, to provide and support the services you've asked for, to send you the monthly tip if you've said yes, and to run our business (for example, invoicing and our tax records).

Your business's systems and customer information

When we set up tools for your business, we may be able to see information about your customers. We only access what we need to do the job, we never sell or share it, and we never use it for our own marketing. Your customer list always belongs to you. Any email list we set up for you only adds customers who have said yes, and every email includes a way to unsubscribe, as the Spam Act 2003 requires.

AI tools

The helpers we set up use business-grade AI tools with data controls, so your information isn't used to train public AI models. We get your written consent before connecting any email inbox or customer system, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only with the service providers we need to run our business, such as Google Workspace (email), Netlify (website), Web3Forms (website forms), Calendly (bookings) and Meta (ad measurement), plus our accounting tools, or when the law requires it. Some of these providers store information on servers outside Australia, such as in the United States. We choose providers with strong security and privacy practices.

Emails from us

We only send our monthly tip to people who have asked for it. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, or by letting us know.

How we keep it safe

We use secure, password-protected accounts with two-step sign-in wherever it's available, and we only keep information for as long as we need it. When a project ends, we remove our access to your systems if you ask us to.

Seeing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, at any time. Just contact us using the details below.

Questions or complaints

If you have a question or a concern, please contact us on 0417 813 548 or jake@secondwindintegration.com. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
